About Blue Orbit

Military-grade leadership discipline, applied to modern engineering.

Pioneer Emporium Solutions, LLC, operating as Blue Orbit, is a small business based in Melbourne, Florida. Led by CEO Jason Sharp, we bring the same standards that govern high-stakes operations into the way organizations design systems, build software, and produce technical drawings.

Company Overview

Discipline is the product, not just the process.

Pioneer Emporium Solutions, LLC, doing business as Blue Orbit, was founded on a simple premise: the standards that keep complex operations safe and effective under pressure are the same standards that produce dependable systems, software, and drawings.

We are a small business headquartered in Melbourne, Florida, led by CEO Jason Sharp. Being small is deliberate: clients work directly with the people doing the work, decisions get made quickly, and nothing is lost in handoffs between account managers and engineers.

Blue Orbit is registered in SAM.gov and is an active, eligible vendor for federal contracting. Our UEI and CAGE codes are available upon request — contact us and we will provide them along with our capability statement.

Our team fuses veteran leadership discipline with modern engineering precision. That means clear scopes, accountable timelines, and deliverables that are built to be maintained, audited, and trusted — not just shipped.

Across mobile app development, full-stack software development, and precision CAD drafting, we hold every engagement to the same standard: understand the mission, execute with integrity, and deliver technical excellence without exception.
